About

The person behind
the work.

A developer who cares about the craft as much as the outcome — clean systems, honest interfaces, and work that says something.

I build things for the web and explain how they work.

I'm Yohane H. Maluwa, a second-year BSc Computer Science student at DMI — St. John the Baptist University in Mangochi, living in Blantyre. My registration number is 24311351028 — and yes, it fits on the first try.

I started taking code seriously when I realised I could stop borrowing interfaces and start making them. Since then I've shipped a terminal-based AI coding agent (hcode), an inventory system for a district council, an explainable machine-learning research project for Malawi's courts, and a full corporate identity for BEAN, an agritech brand — all documented on my GitHub.

Alongside coursework I earned a Huawei ICT certification at national level (2024), interned in the ICT department at BTDC, and spend weekends tutoring classmates through data structures. My inbox is always open — whether it's an internship, a collaboration or a hard question.
