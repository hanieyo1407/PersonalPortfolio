Bean®

“Bean has always been there.” — a complete corporate identity package for an agritech brand.

View full image ↗
The brief

Build a corporate identity from zero for BEAN — an organic seed company supplying legumes to Malawian farmers. The identity had to feel agricultural but modern: a letterform grown from a bean seed, a burgundy-and-charcoal palette with one eco-green signal colour, and a tagline simple enough for a farmer to remember.

Problem solved

The gap: agricultural brands in Malawi default to clip-art and cluttered layouts, so they read as cheap. BEAN's system fixes that with a single organic letterform, a strict three-colour palette (#4A1521 · #1A1A1A · #4CAF50), and consistent typography applied across every touchpoint — from a seed tin to a highway billboard.

Deliverables
  • LogoOrganic seed-built letterform, maroon + black versions
  • StationeryBusiness cards — front: logo & slogan, back: contact block
  • MerchandiseSeed tin, pickup-truck livery, feather flags, t-shirts
  • Out-of-homeFolded posters and a full roadside billboard
  • TypeCustom display sans + "ohno softy bold" body face

  • (006)

    judicially-mw

    Research · Explainable ML →
    What it is

    Academic research project: an explainable machine-learning system that prioritises court cases for Malawi's judiciary. Supervised by Mr. Joseph Mbando.

    Problem solved: case backlogs bury urgent matters. The system reads 682 judgments (2010–2019) from the Malawi Supreme Court of Appeal corpus, ranks cases into High / Medium / Low priority tiers with a Random Forest classifier, and shows its reasoning through SHAP values so a registrar can audit every decision. NLP preprocessing via spaCy and NLTK.

    • Python
    • Flask 3.0
    • scikit-learn
    • SHAP
    • spaCy / NLTK
    • MWCC corpus
